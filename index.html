xDailyBench: Benchmarking LLMs on Professional Consultation for Real-Life Problems

1ByteDance Seed    2M-A-P    3TokenWave.AI

  • What is xDailyBench： It covers 56 everyday scenarios and 248 authentic, open-ended tasks spanning personal life, work, and study, evaluating end-to-end agent performance on real-world everyday needs under realistic user constraints.
  • Why xDailyBench： We address the limitations of existing exam-style and professional workflow benchmarks, which often fail to capture ambiguous, personalized, context-dependent requests with no single ground-truth answer.
  • How xDailyBench is Built： It integrates real-user tasks, criterion-based binary rubrics covering both explicit and implicit requirements, and a unified taxonomy for evaluating agent capabilities.
  • What Insight We Find： Evaluation across 11 frontier models shows that the best-performing model achieves only 75.6% task-level performance, while all models exhibit over 9 percentage-point gaps on implicit requirements, highlighting intent inference as a persistent bottleneck for reliable task completion.

Construction Pipeline

The construction of xDailyBench follows a rigorous, multi-stage pipeline that integrates real-user contributions, AI-assisted review, and iterative human quality control. Tasks are contributed by participants with practical experience using AI tools recruited through a crowdsourcing platform, who submit scenarios identical or similar to those they genuinely encounter in their personal lives, professional workflows, or studies.

Failure Analysis

By jointly examining generated artifacts, rubric losses, and execution traces, xDailyBench identifies two core failure mechanisms. Both can involve substantial agent activity, yet they break down at different stages of producing a reliable result.

01 Feasibility judgment

Detailed Planning without a Feasibility Check

The agent moves directly into detailed planning when constraints on space, budget, schedule, work limits, and risk are scattered across the prompt and attachments, but never verifies that all conditions can hold simultaneously.

Scattered constraints→ Detailed plan produced→ No joint feasibility check
Paper case · Older Home Renovation Plan
  • The proposed 14-week schedule exceeds the three-month move-in deadline.
  • Only RMB 10,000 remains for contingencies, while a low attic is assigned as a treatment room.
  • The claimed on-time, on-budget delivery is therefore unsupported.
02 Stopping judgment

Evidence-Collection Non-convergence

Because open-ended information tasks have no natural retrieval endpoint, the agent mistakes successful search calls for progress and keeps adding queries instead of deciding that the evidence is sufficient and moving to synthesis.

Relevant source found→ More searches added→ No synthesis or deliverable
Paper case · Data-breach Legal Analysis
  • The official source ranks first in the initial search, but the agent never opens it.
  • Query growth adds volume without new evidence; the last 60 searches yield only nine unique top-three URLs.
